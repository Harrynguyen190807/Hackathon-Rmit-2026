## Project Overview
1. Vision & originality:
VitaTrack transforms daily health management by bridging continuous biometric tracking with intelligent prescription conflict analysis. Unlike conventional health apps that expose intimate health histories to centralized servers, VitaTrack ensures individuals maintain sovereign ownership of their medical records.

2. Trust constraint insight:
Medical prescriptions and real-time biometrics represent people's most private data. Today, users avoid sharing prescriptions with AI platforms due to surveillance and data leakage risks. Removing this privacy barrier unlocks automated, real-time drug interaction warnings without sacrificing confidentiality.

3. Value & impact:
Chronic disease patients and polypharmacy users gain instant safety. A patient taking multiple concurrent medications avoids potentially fatal drug-drug interactions through edge-encrypted prescription scanning, preventing avoidable hospitalizations and adverse complications.

4. Why TideCloak matters:
TideCloak enforces edge encryption and zero-knowledge identity verification. Sensitive health vitals and prescription OCR outputs are encrypted at the source; third-party AI models only receive sanitized, zero-trust proofs, making mass data harvesting technically impossible.

5. Prototype & storytelling:
The working prototype demonstrates a seamless user journey: (1) Synced health telemetry dashboard, (2) Prescription OCR scanner with automated drug interaction alerts secured by TideCloak, (3) Confidential AI consultation, and (4) Smart medication reminders.
